== SUMMARY (full) ==

Velum is a compliance kit that makes OpenZeppelin's Confidential Token usable by a
regulated fund. It wires together two modules the library ships separately, and adds
the piece neither provides.

Compliance asks two questions. WHO MAY HOLD? Upstream answers with a flat address
allowlist. velum-policy replaces it: the token's authorization gate consults an
ERC-3643/T-REX identity registry, so a holder must carry a valid qualified-investor
claim issued by an approved certifier. HOW MUCH MAY THEY HOLD? That is structurally
impossible at the same interface - it receives an address and never a value, because
balances are Pedersen commitments. So Velum moves the check to the only party that can
make it: the holder proves "my position is at least T" using disclose_balance_ge, a
predicate circuit upstream specifies (SELECTIVE_DISCLOSURE.md section 9) but does not
ship, and velum-attest verifies that proof on-chain - a component the same spec marks
out of scope. Nobody learns the amount. The verifier included.

This is the sub-lane's RWA / permissioned token deliverable: a compliant asset built on
OZ's RWA and access-control modules with policy enforcement - extended with the
quantitative half that interface cannot express. Everything runs on an unmodified
upstream token, in Rust/Soroban and Noir/UltraHonk.

The kit also answers upstream's open question on individual clawback (COMPLIANCE.md
section 5): premise verified, seize circuit built and proven.

Anchor case: a Brazilian receivables fund (FIDC) under CVM 175. MIT, testnet only - the
UltraHonk verifier is developer preview and unaudited.


== SUMMARY (short, ~60 words) ==

Velum makes OpenZeppelin's Confidential Token usable by a regulated fund. velum-policy
swaps the flat address allowlist for an ERC-3643/T-REX identity registry - a holder needs
a valid qualified-investor claim. And because the policy interface never sees a value,
Velum moves the quantitative check to the holder: they prove "my position >= T" and a
contract verifies it on-chain, without anyone learning the amount.


== ONE-LINE ==

Identity-gated confidential RWA on Stellar, plus on-chain proof that a position clears a
regulatory threshold - without revealing the amount to anyone, the verifier included.
