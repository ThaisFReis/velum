Velum.

Holder-attested compliance for Confidential Tokens on Stellar.

A regulated fund can hide its register of holders — and still prove it obeys the rules. Without revealing the amount to anyone. Including the verifier.

github.com/ThaisFReis/velum

The problem

Tokenizing a fund makes it less private
than the paperwork it replaces.

In the filing cabinet

The register of holders sits with the administrator. Who subscribed, how much, when they left — none of it is public.

On a public ledger

All of it is. Ten institutional positions de-anonymize by correlating subscription timing with off-chain events — and the information has a paying audience.

Confidential Tokens fix the balances. They do not fix compliance.

02

Where it breaks

Compliance has two halves. Upstream ships
the primitives for both, wired to neither.

The questionUpstream todayWhat a regulated fund needs
Who may hold? A flat address allowlist. No reason, no expiry, no attesting party. A valid claim — qualified investor — issued by an approved certifier. Their issue #766. Still open.
How much may they hold? Structurally impossible. is_authorized(account, token) -> bool sees no value. Balances are commitments. Minimum ticket. Concentration ceiling. Per-investor cap. Every rule that mentions a number.
03

The insight

So the check has to move.

Under FHE, a contract compares ciphertexts with no witness — the check fits inside the transfer. Under Pedersen commitments, a proof needs a witness. And for any balance, exactly one party holds it.

A sender cannot prove anything about the recipient's balance. It has no opening for it — and binding guarantees it cannot find another.

Quantitative compliance cannot be transaction-gated.
It must be holder-attested — the party that can prove
is the party whose balance it is.

04

What we built · on an unmodified upstream token

Four pieces.

ComponentWhat it does
velum-policy The token's gate asks an ERC-3643/T-REX identity registry instead of a list. One KYC registry governs the public asset and the confidential wrapper.
disclose_balance_ge The predicate circuit upstream specifies and does not ship. The holder proves "my position ≥ T". The proof's validity is the assertion — there is nothing to decrypt.
velum-attest On-chain verification — which the same spec marks out of scope. Every public input is read from the token and the profile; the prover supplies only the proof.
circuits/seize Answers their open question on individual clawback: premise verified, circuit built, and a partial seizure verified on-chain.
05

The demo

Three things you can watch happen.

01 The explorer goes blind

Addresses visible, amounts encrypted. The register stops being public.

02 A wallet without a claim is refused

Not because it is on a list — because it carries no qualified-investor attestation.

03 A holder proves the threshold

A contract verifies it on-chain. Nobody learns the amount — the verifier included.

And a false claim? It cannot even be constructed. The witness fails before a proof exists.

07

Why believe the problem is real

Because they wrote it down.

COMPLIANCE.md:218 — OpenZeppelin, on why individual clawback is unsolved:

"Because the clawback circuit does not have access to vk_A… The follow-up revision will pin down whether r_s is supplied as a private witness with an auxiliary opening proof or derived in-circuit from a separately escrowed value."

They name the obstacle and list the remedy. We take the second option and say what to escrow: vk_A itself — then verify it in code.

Every claim we make about their product is sourced to a file and a line in Appendix A. Trait signatures and verbatim quotes — not our characterisation.

08

What we are not claiming

The honest part.

Not production

Developer-preview primitives; the UltraHonk verifier is unaudited. Testnet only, no real value.

Not finished

The clawback migration — a register-circuit change and re-registration — is priced, not built.

Not solved

Predicates over encrypted aggregates across accounts — concentration by tranche, subordination ratio between senior and subordinated — remain open. As far as we can determine, on any chain.

Not invented

We implement their specification. The novelty claim is bounded: no public implementation exists in any repository, branch, tag or history.

09

Where this goes

"Regulated" and "public"
stop being opposites.

The anchor case is a Brazilian receivables fund under CVM 175 — senior and subordinated classes, a register that is public today. The kit serves any regulated issuer with the same paradox.
